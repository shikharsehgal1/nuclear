## 1. What U.UN and U.U represent

**One class of trust units; two TSX trading lines (currencies), not separate classes.**

- The 2026 Shelf: units are "transferable, non-redeemable trust units … Each trust unit represents an equal, fractional, undivided ownership interest in the net assets of the Trust attributable to the particular class of trust units. To date, the Trust has issued only one class or series of trust units." It also states: "The trust units are listed and posted for trading on the Toronto Stock Exchange (the 'TSX') under the symbols 'U.UN' (Canadian dollar denominated) and 'U.U' (U.S. dollar denominated)." (2026 Shelf, cover pages; retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- Single security identifiers for both lines — CUSIP 85210A104, ISIN CA85210A1049 (Factsheet, June 30, 2026; retrieved 2026-07-20). One CUSIP/ISIN across the two lines is the structural signature of a single fungible security trading on two currency lines.
- Fungibility/journaling: the Sprott FAQ states investors can "reclassify their shares to $US," i.e., holdings can be journaled between the CAD and USD lines through a broker (FAQ; retrieved 2026-07-20). The FAQ describes the two tickers as "two currency options (Canadian dollars and U.S. dollars)". The exact broker mechanics (fees, timing) are not published by Sprott — **UNVERIFIED** at the operational level.
- Settlement currencies: U.UN trades and settles in CAD; U.U trades and settles in USD (2026 Shelf cover, quoting closing prices "Cdn$29.91 (U.UN) and U.S.$21.65 (U.U)" on January 20, 2026; retrieved 2026-07-20). The Trust's functional/presentation currency and published NAV are USD (2025 Annual Report, Note 3, "Functional and presentation currency"; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- OTC symbol: **SRUUF** exists as a US OTC line quoted in USD; confirmed by Yahoo Finance (https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SRUUF/), TradingView (https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/OTC-SRUUF/), MarketBeat short-interest page (https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/OTCMKTS/SRUUF/short-interest/), all retrieved 2026-07-20. Note: Sprott's own factsheet and fund page list only U.U and U.UN and do **not** advertise an OTC ticker; SRUUF appears to be an OTC market quotation of the same units (typical unsponsored cross-listing). Whether SRUUF is a sponsored line — **UNVERIFIED**.

## 2. Redemption mechanics — THERE IS NO REDEMPTION

This is the single most important structural fact for discount modeling, and it is unambiguous in primary sources:

- 2026 Shelf, cover: the Trust may offer "up to U.S.$2,000,000,000 of transferable, **non-redeemable** trust units." (Retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- OSC Decision (July 12, 2021), para. 27: "**The Trust does not currently offer a redemption feature. Unitholders will not be permitted to redeem their Units for either cash or physical Uranium following the completion of the Arrangement.**" (https://www.osc.ca/en/securities-law/orders-rulings-decisions/sprott-asset-management-lp-and-sprott-physical-uranium-trust, retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- The Trust is a "non-redeemable investment fund" as defined in the Securities Act (Ontario) and is regulated under NI 81-102 as such (OSC Decision, paras. 18, 26; 2026 Shelf "Investment and Operating Restrictions"; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- A full-text search of the 2026 Shelf finds **no** redemption provision of any kind; the only occurrences of "redeem" are in "non-redeemable."

**Contrast with other Sprott physical trusts.** Sprott Physical Gold Trust (PHYS) and siblings allow monthly cash redemption at 95% of the lesser of market price and NAV, plus physical redemption for large holders (see e.g. Sprott Physical Gold Trust AIF filings, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477049/000199937125002923/ex99-5.htm, retrieved 2026-07-20). **SPUT does not have this feature.** The provisional assumption that SPUT has a monthly cash redemption at a discount to NAV is **wrong** — there is no unitholder-initiated redemption at any haircut.

- Issuer buyback / tender: no normal-course issuer bid (NCIB), substantial issuer bid, or tender program was found in the 2026 Shelf, ATM Supplement, 2025 Annual Report, FAQ, or press-release list as of 2026-07-20. The 2025 Annual Report's capital-management note contains generic language about the "ability to issue, repurchase and resell Units" with "no legal restrictions on the issue, repurchase or resale of such Units beyond those included in its trust agreement" (2025 Annual Report, "Capital management"; retrieved 2026-07-20) — i.e., a buyback is legally possible but none has been instituted. Existence of any active buyback: **none found; absence beyond the documents reviewed is UNVERIFIED**.

**Modeling consequence:** there is no arbitrage mechanism, at any haircut, that converts units into NAV. The discount has no contractual floor; only ATM issuance caps the premium side (Section 4).

## 3. NAV calculation and publication

- **Time and frequency:** "The value of the net assets of the Trust and the net asset value for a particular class … are determined **daily as of 4:00 p.m., Toronto time, on each business day** by the Trust's valuation agent, which is RBC Investor Services. … the term 'business day' refers to any day on which the TSX is open for trading." The Manager may also compute NAV at other times it deems appropriate. (2026 Shelf, "Calculating Net Asset Value"; retrieved 2026-07-20.) The FAQ concurs: "The Trust posts a NAV daily, at the close of each trading day." (FAQ, retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **Definition:** net assets = aggregate fair market value of assets minus fair value of liabilities (excluding liabilities represented by units); NAV per unit = class net assets / units outstanding (2026 Shelf, same section). NAV is computed and published in USD (2025 Annual Report, Note 3; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- **Publication:** posted daily on the Sprott fund page (fund page shows NAV, market price, premium/discount, and pounds of U3O8 daily; e.g., as of July 17, 2026: NAV US$20.59, market price US$18.34, discount −10.92%, 81,447,348 lb U3O8; retrieved 2026-07-20). Factsheet: "Transparent Daily Reporting of Net Asset Value (NAV) and Holdings." (Retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **Which uranium price enters NAV:** the audited financial statements say only: "NAV is defined as the Trust's net assets … calculated using the value of physical uranium based on the **end-of-day price provided by widely recognized pricing sources**," and uranium is "measured at fair value determined by reference to **published price quotations**," classified **Level 2** in the fair-value hierarchy (2025 Annual Report, Notes 3, 5 and "Net Asset Value"; retrieved 2026-07-20). Separately, the Sprott fund page/factsheet describe the *performance benchmark* as "a proprietary composite of U3O8 spot prices from **TradeTech, UxC, S&P Platts and Numerco**" (fund page and Factsheet footnote, retrieved 2026-07-20). The precise identity/weighting of the pricing source(s) RBC uses for the daily 4 p.m. NAV (single provider vs. the composite) is **not publicly disclosed — UNVERIFIED**. Update frequency of the inputs: UxC's Ux U3O8 spot price has been published **daily** (business days) since September 2021, previously weekly (UxC, https://www.uxc.com/p/data/about-uranium-prices, retrieved 2026-07-20); TradeTech publishes daily/weekly indicators; Numerco publishes live/intraday indications (Section 8).
- **Trade-date vs settlement-date; unsettled purchases:** "Investment transactions in physical uranium are accounted for on the **trade date basis**." Realized/unrealized gains use weighted-average cost. Receivables such as "due from broker" are carried at amortized cost; other liabilities at amortized cost. Credit risk on unsettled uranium transactions is managed by dealing with counterparties "the Manager believes to be creditworthy" (2025 Annual Report, Notes 3 and financial-risk note; retrieved 2026-07-20). So uranium bought but not yet delivered/settled is already in NAV at fair value from trade date, with the payable/receivable carried against it.
- **Cash drag:** the investment restrictions require ≥90% of total net assets in Uranium and ≤10% in cash/short-term government or investment-grade paper, **"except during the 60-day period following the closing of additional offerings"** (2026 Shelf, Investment and Operating Restrictions (a); retrieved 2026-07-20) — i.e., ATM proceeds may lawfully sit in cash for up to 60 days before deployment.
- The Trust is unleveraged; borrowing limited to ≤10% of net assets under NI 81-102 and used only for short-term trade settlement, with no borrowing arrangements in place (2026 Shelf, "Borrowing Arrangements"; retrieved 2026-07-20).

## 4. ATM program (at-the-market issuance)

- **Current shelf:** US$2.0B base shelf prospectus dated January 22, 2026, effective 25 months (2026 Shelf cover; retrieved 2026-07-20). **Current ATM supplement:** up to **US$1.0B** of units through agents Cantor Fitzgerald Canada, Virtu Canada, Canaccord Genuity, and BMO Nesbitt Burns under an amended and restated sales agreement dated December 6, 2024; sales made as "at-the-market distributions" (NI 44-102) directly on the TSX or other Canadian marketplaces at prevailing market prices (ATM Supplement, cover and Plan of Distribution; retrieved 2026-07-20). Historical note: the original 2021 ATM was launched with an initial US$300M and repeatedly upsized (aggregate shelf capacity across 2021–2026 supplements has been in the billions of USD) — precise sequence of every past supplement **UNVERIFIED** here; the current live capacity is the US$1.0B supplement under the US$2.0B shelf.
- **Issuance floor (the key asymmetry):** "In accordance with paragraph 9.3(2) of NI 81-102, the issue price of the trust units will not (a) as far as reasonably practicable, be a price that causes dilution of the NAV of the Trust's other outstanding securities at the time of issue and **(b) be a price that is less than the most recently calculated NAV per trust unit.** Accordingly, the trust units sold pursuant to the offering will not be sold at an issue price that is less than 100% of the most recently calculated NAV per trust unit immediately prior to, or upon, the determination of the pricing of such issuance." (2026 Shelf "Plan of Distribution" and Investment and Operating Restrictions (c); identical language in ATM Supplement; retrieved 2026-07-20.) This is both a trust-agreement restriction and a securities-law requirement for non-redeemable funds.
- **Premium behavior:** "The offering and the use of the net proceeds from the offering are **intended to be accretive to NAV per trust unit** and are intended to lead to an increase in physical Uranium owned per unitholder on an overall basis." The Manager is reimbursed for drawdown expenses "only to the extent there is a **sufficient premium** as determined by the Manager between the NAV per trust unit and the market price at which each such trust unit is sold." "Due to the nature of the 'at-the-market' offering, the Manager will be able to utilize the program immediately or whenever it deems it appropriate." (ATM Supplement, cover and "Use of Proceeds"; retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **Discount behavior:** when units trade below the last calculated NAV, ATM sales are mechanically impossible (cannot sell below 100% of NAV). There is **no symmetric mechanism** (no redemption, no automatic buyback) operating at a discount — supply of units is one-way ratchet upward. (2026 Shelf; OSC Decision para. 27; retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **Use of proceeds / timing of uranium purchases:** "The net proceeds will be used by the Trust to acquire physical Uranium" (U3O8 and UF6 both permitted) (ATM Supplement, "Use of Proceeds"; retrieved 2026-07-20). Purchases are at the Manager's discretion and proceeds may remain in cash/short-term instruments for up to 60 days after a closing (2026 Shelf, restriction (a)); there is **no covenant requiring same-day purchase**. Empirically SPUT has often deployed quickly, but that is behavior, not obligation — deployment-lag statistics **UNVERIFIED** here.
- **Costs:** Agents receive "a cash fee of up to **3.0%** of the aggregate gross proceeds" of ATM sales (ATM Supplement cover; retrieved 2026-07-20). The Manager receives "(i) a **commission of 1.0% of the gross value of any purchases or sales of Uranium**" (paid within 10 business days of delivery), with the Manager responsible for third-party brokerage fees; plus "Additional Fees" of generally $25,000–$75,000 per transaction for certain value-added services (2026 Shelf, "Fees and Expenses"; retrieved 2026-07-20). So the believed "~1% on uranium purchases" is **confirmed at exactly 1.0%**, payable to the Manager.
- **Management fee:** "a monthly management fee equal to **1/12 of 0.35% of the NAV** of the Trust plus applicable taxes … calculated and accrued daily and payable monthly in arrears" (2026 Shelf, "Fees and Expenses"; Factsheet: "Annual management fee of 0.35% per annum, plus operating expenses"; retrieved 2026-07-20). The Sprott fund page displays an all-in MER of ~0.68% (fund page, retrieved 2026-07-20; figure as reported by Sprott — components not broken out there).
- **Scale of issuance (recent):** 41,201,924 units issued for gross proceeds of $762.6M in 2025 vs. 14,097,400 units / $310.6M in 2024; 318,448,764 units outstanding and total NAV US$6.616B as of January 22, 2026 (2025 Annual Report MRFP; ATM Supplement "Capitalization"; retrieved 2026-07-20).

## 6. CME UxC U3O8 futures (UX)

- **Spec:** COMEX-listed (transferred from NYMEX DCM to COMEX DCM effective July 12, 2020), contract unit **250 pounds of U3O8**, quoted in USD/lb, **financially settled** — no physical delivery. (CME contract specs, https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/metals/other/uranium.contractSpecs.html; CME Rulebook Chapter 970, https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/COMEX/9/970.pdf; UxC overview, https://www.uxc.com/p/data/cme?print=true&pdf=true; all retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **Settlement basis:** floating price historically was the month-end Ux U3O8 spot price; "Commencing with the October 2024 contract month and beyond, the Exchange will utilize the **arithmetic average of all weekly spot U3O8 prices published by UxC, LLC during each contract month in the Ux Weekly**" (CME Market Regulation notice MSN06-12-24B, https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/market-regulation/2024/06/MSN06-12-24B.html; retrieved 2026-07-20). Trading terminates on the last Monday of the contract month that is a business day (CME Rulebook Ch. 970; retrieved 2026-07-20). Position limit 2,000 contracts (500,000 lb) per month (UxC/CME FAQ, https://www.uxc.com/docs/p/cme/Uranium_FAQsPRESS.pdf; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- **Liquidity — thin to nonexistent.** UxC's own CME overview noted "There is no open interest in October 2024 contract month and beyond" at its publication date, and TradingView showed front-contract open interest of 0 (https://www.tradingview.com/symbols/COMEX-UX1!/, retrieved 2026-07-20). The contract trades sporadically via Globex/ClearPort blocks; it is a hedging/marking instrument, not a liquid trading venue. **Do not treat UX as an executable hedge leg without checking live OI/volume; assume near-zero screen liquidity.** (Sprott's own options press release notes "the absence of an active uranium futures market," retrieved 2026-07-20.)

## 7. History: UPC → SPUT (regime break on 2021-07-19)

- Uranium Participation Corporation (TSX: **U**) was a publicly listed **corporation** (reclassified from a non-redeemable investment fund to a corporate issuer on February 22, 2013), managed by **Denison Mines Inc.** under a management services agreement (OSC Decision paras. 2–3; Denison press releases, https://denisonmines.com/news/denison-announces-five-year-management-services-ag-122680/ and https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2016/03/04/1299626/0/en/Uranium-Participation-Corporation-Announces-Execution-of-Three-Year-Management-Services-Agreement-With-Denison.html; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- UPC held **both U3O8 and UF6**, roughly 98% of it indirectly through Bermuda subsidiaries (UPBL, UPBL2) as of May 31, 2021 (OSC Decision paras. 1–5; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- Plan of arrangement (arrangement agreement dated April 27, 2021) under s.182 OBCA: **closed July 19, 2021**; UPC shareholders received **0.5 SPUT units per UPC share**; the Trust began trading as U.UN (CAD) and U.U (USD); the transaction added ~US$630M to Sprott's AUM; Sprott paid a CAD$5.8M termination fee to the former manager (Denison) (Sprott completion press release, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/07/19/2264707/35318/en/Sprott-Announces-Completion-of-the-Arrangement-of-Uranium-Participation-Corporation-to-Form-the-Sprott-Physical-Uranium-Trust.html; Sprott 6-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512920/000110465921093585/tm2122541d1_ex99-1.htm; OSC Decision; all retrieved 2026-07-20). SPUT factsheet inception date: July 19, 2021 (retrieved 2026-07-20).
- **What changed structurally:**
  - **No ATM under UPC.** UPC raised capital through episodic bought-deal/marketed equity offerings; SPUT's continuous ATM (launched shortly after conversion) is new and is the primary premium-capping mechanism. (2026 Shelf/ATM Supplement describe the ATM regime; UPC had none — corroborated by UPC's financing history; retrieved 2026-07-20.)
  - **Different legal form and manager:** Ontario corporation managed by Denison → closed-end trust managed by Sprott Asset Management LP, trustee/valuation agent RBC Investor Services (OSC Decision paras. 18–26; retrieved 2026-07-20).
  - **Holdings:** UPC held U3O8 **and UF6**; SPUT's mandate permits both ("uranium oxide in concentrates and uranium hexafluoride," 2026 Shelf cover) but current holdings and reporting are effectively all U3O8 (fund page and 2025 Annual Report report pounds of U3O8 only; retrieved 2026-07-20).
  - **Different disclosure cadence:** UPC published NAV monthly (as a corporation, at month-end); SPUT publishes daily NAV. Monthly-NAV claim for UPC: consistent with its corporate-issuer practice but **UNVERIFIED** against a UPC filing in this pass.
- **Modeling consequence:** pre-2021-07-19 data (TSX: U) reflects a different vehicle: no ATM premium cap, monthly-ish NAV transparency, mixed U3O8/UF6 inventory, corporate overhead — premium/discount dynamics from the UPC era (which saw persistent double-digit discounts) are **not** drawn from the same regime as SPUT-era data.

## 8. What "uranium spot" actually is

- **There is no exchange.** UxC: "There is no formal exchange for uranium as there is for other commodities such as gold or oil." Cameco: "Uranium does not trade on an open market like other commodities. Buyers and sellers negotiate contracts privately." (UxC, https://www.uxc.com/p/data/about-uranium-prices; Cameco, https://www.cameco.com/invest/markets/uranium-price; both retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **UxC:** the Ux U3O8 Price is an **assessment** — "based on the most competitive offer of which UxC is aware," with a 2:30 p.m. ET cutoff, and "not necessarily based on completed transactions." Published **daily** (business days) since September 2021, weekly before that. UxC also publishes Broker Average Bid/Offer computed from best spot bids/offers reported by two participating brokers, **Evolution Markets and Numerco** (UxC, https://www.uxc.com/p/data/about-uranium-prices and https://www.uxc.com/p/data/broker-overview; retrieved 2026-07-20.)
- **TradeTech** publishes competing daily/weekly spot value assessments of the same brokered OTC market (TradeTech data as cited in Sprott factsheet performance notes, retrieved 2026-07-20; methodology detail **UNVERIFIED** — subscription).
- **Numerco** disseminates live/intraday spot and forward bid/offer indications for the nuclear fuel cycle via web/app/daily email — the closest thing to an intraday quote, but still broker indications, not firm exchange quotes (https://numerco.com/products-services/nuclear-fuel/ and https://numerco.com/IOI/; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- **Cameco** republishes **month-end industry averages**: "Cameco calculates industry average prices from the month-end prices published by UxC and TradeTech" (spot and long-term; long-term pre-May-2004 from TradeTech only), updated monthly (https://www.cameco.com/invest/markets/uranium-price; retrieved 2026-07-20).
- **Consequence:** every "uranium spot price" input (including whatever feeds SPUT's NAV) is an assessed reference price for lot-sized (typically 100k lb) brokered deliveries at specific facilities — **not an executable continuous price**. NAV itself is therefore an estimate marked to an assessment (uranium is Level 2 in SPUT's fair-value hierarchy — 2025 Annual Report Note 5, retrieved 2026-07-20). Basis between assessments (UxC vs TradeTech vs Numerco intraday) is itself a source of NAV noise.

## Observed premium/discount behavior (context)

- As of 2026-07-17: NAV US$20.59 vs market price US$18.34 → **−10.92% discount** (Sprott fund page, retrieved 2026-07-20).
- FY2025: units "traded on the TSX at an average discount to net asset value of approximately **5.7%**" (2025 Annual Report MRFP, retrieved 2026-07-20) — while the Trust simultaneously issued $762.6M of new units during premium windows in the same year. Discounts of ~10%+ have persisted for months with no forced closure mechanism.
